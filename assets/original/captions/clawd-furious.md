# clawd-furious.svg

An animation of Clawd trembling with rage, stomping on the ground, and spewing fire from the top of his head. 3s+ loop, with multiple durations layered together.

## Body
Standard torso. body-tremble 0.12s linear high-frequency micro-shake translate; body-hunch 1.5s scale(1.02~1.04, 0.94~0.96) + translateY(0.5~1px) tense hunched shoulders. The eyes scaleY(0.6) into an angry frowning glare, with a brief scaleY(0.05) eye-closing instant at 91-94%. Both arms tremble at high frequency at 0.1s. The legs (outer/inner) stomp out of phase at 0.7s ease-out.

- **Facial flush**: A red (#D32F2F) overlay layer pulsing at opacity 0~0.38
- **Flame particles**: 6 particles (#FF3D00/#FF6D00/#FFC107) rising from the top of the head, shrinking via scale and fading out via opacity, staggered with delays of 0/0.15/0.3/0.1/0.25/0.4s
