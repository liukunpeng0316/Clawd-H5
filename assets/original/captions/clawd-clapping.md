# clawd-clapping.svg

Clawd alternately clapping left and right in celebration, 3.5s loop.

## Body
Standard torso with breathing (3.2s). Legs extended to 1x4 (y=11), with a -1.5px bounce when clapping. Both arms have a hand shadow (black, 0.25 opacity). Two sets of eyes: normal vertical-bar eyes (at rest) and smiling eyes ^^ (while clapping), toggled via opacity.

## Animation
- **Body sway (clap-bob, 3.5s)**: 25-47% tilt left (-1px) with bounce → 50% center → 53-69% tilt right (+1px) with bounce → 74% center
- **Left arm clap (arm-l, 3.5s)**: rest → move in (+2px) → two claps on the left (+4.5px) → transition → two claps on the right (+8.5px) → retract
- **Right arm clap (arm-r, 3.5s)**: mirrored motion, left side (-8.5px) → right side (-4.5px)
- **Legs bounce (legs-bob, 3.5s)**: -1.5px upward bounce on each clap
- **Eye toggle**: 0-22% normal eyes → 27-73% smiling eyes → 80-100% normal eyes
- **Sparks (sp1/sp2/sp3)**: 4 bursts (35%/43% on the left, 57%/65% on the right), gold (#FFC107/#FFD700/#FFF59D)

## Expression
Clawd happily claps left and right in turn, the body swaying and bouncing side to side with the clapping rhythm, with gold sparks bursting at each clap.
