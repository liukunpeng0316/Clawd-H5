# clawd-curious.svg

Clawd curiously tilting its head in thought, 4s loop, with the right arm raised near the chin.

## Body
Standard torso with breathing. 18-70% the body rotate(4~5°) tilts to the right, cocking the head in thought. Two sets of eyes: normal eyes and "innocent eyes" (rounded eyes with white highlights); switches to the innocent eyes after 15%. The right arm rotate(-30~-35°) raises near the chin in a thinking pose.

## Props
None. The curiosity is conveyed solely through the head tilt, eye switch, and raised right arm.

## Animation
- **curious-tilt (4s)**: body rotate 0→±5° head tilt
- **eyes-norm-vis / eyes-inno-vis (4s)**: the two eye sets toggle via mutually exclusive opacity, blinking into the innocent eyes at 15%
- **arm-r-raise (4s)**: 20-68% right arm rotate(-35°) raised
- **shadow-tilt (4s)**: the shadow tilts in sync with the body

## Expression
Clawd curiously tilts its head to the right, switches to big innocent eyes with highlights, and raises its right hand to rest gently against its chin.
