# clawd-working-wizard.svg

Clawd transforms into a wizard casting a spell.

## Body
Standard torso and four legs. It wears a deep purple wizard hat (#673AB7 triangle + #512DA8 brim + #FFC107 golden eight-pointed star badge).

## Props
- **Wizard hat**: a deep purple triangle, with a darker purple rectangle brim and a golden eight-pointed star in the middle
- **Staff**: a brown shaft (#8D6E63, 1x6) + a golden orb at the top (#FFD700, 1x1)

## Animation
- **Left arm (3s)**: a large 20°-120° swing
- **Right arm holding staff (3s)**: a large -20° to -120° waving
- **Eyes**: start squinted (scaleY 0.1), fully open at 45-95% (concentrating on the spell)
- **Magic starbursts**: four eight-pointed stars (gold #FFD700 / blue #40C4FF / purple #B388FF / pink #FF8A80), on a 2-second cycle flying up from the staff tip to y=-15px, enlarging 1.5x and rotating 180° before fading out
- **Body float (3s)**: translateY -2px, hovering slightly

## Expression
Clawd, wearing a wizard hat, waves its staff, magic starbursts erupting, body hovering slightly — a spell in progress.
