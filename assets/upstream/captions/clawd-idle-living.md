# clawd-idle-living.svg

Clawd's richest single-file idle animation, a full 16-second loop.

## Body
Fully upright. Standard torso, four legs (each 1x3, y=12), both arms (each 2x2), both eyes (each 1x2). Extra part: a teardrop (blue #40C4FF, 1x1). The yawn is expressed through body stretching, arms spreading open, eyes closing, and the teardrop.

## 16-Second Animation Sequence
1. **0-8% idle breathing**: only the 3.2-second breathing loop runs.
2. **12-22% glancing right**: the torso shifts 1px right, the eyes shift 3px right (parallax).
3. **30-36% scratching head**: the left arm quickly rises to rotate(35°) and springs back and forth 3 times.
4. **42-50% glancing left**: the torso shifts 1px left, the eyes shift 3px left.
5. **60-76% yawning**: the torso stretches vertically (scale 0.9,1.1) → arms spread open to rotate(±80°) → eyes close → teardrop slides down → settles with a compression.
6. **80-100% returns to idle**.

## Expression
Looking around, scratching its head out of boredom, letting out a big yawn with watering eyes — a complex idle animation full of lifelike detail.
