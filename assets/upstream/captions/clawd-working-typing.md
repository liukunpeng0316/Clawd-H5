# clawd-working-typing.svg

Clawd typing furiously at high speed.

## Body
Standard torso and four legs stay still. Eyes shrunk to 1x1 (focused squint). The body has an extremely faint tremor on a 0.08s cycle.

## Props
- **Laptop**: gray base (#546E7A) + screen back (#78909C, 9x5.5) + white pixel logo (1x1) + blue glow (#40C4FF)
- **Data particles**: seven light-blue (#40C4FF, 1.5x1.5)

## Animation
- **Both arms typing**: left arm 0.15s (70°-110°), right arm faster at 0.12s (-80° to -110°), extremely high speed
- **Eye scan (1.2s)**: 5-step stepped movement (x from -2 to +2px), with an instant jump back at the end (simulating a carriage return at the end of a line)
- **Logo breathing (1.5s)**: opacity 0.4-1.0
- **Data particles**: each floats up 15px and fades out on a 1s cycle

## Expression
Clawd hammers the keyboard furiously in front of a laptop, its eyes scanning code rapidly, data particles pouring out from the screen.
