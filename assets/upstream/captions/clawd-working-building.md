# clawd-working-building.svg

Clawd is forging and building, with a 1-second loop of hammer-strike bounces.

## Body
Standard torso. The eyes are standard 1-wide black rectangles at (4,8.5)/(10,8.5) (here height 1.5, for a hard squinting look). It wears a yellow hard hat (#FBC02D/#F9A825 multi-layered rectangles). The right arm grips a pixel hammer (brown handle #795548 + gray head #9E9E9E).

## Props
- **Hard hat**: multiple layers of light and dark yellow rectangles, covering the top of the head down to y=5, with a raised ridge decoration on top
- **Hammer**: a brown vertical-bar handle + a gray horizontal-block head
- **Anvil**: a blue-gray base (#546E7A), with hot metal on top (flashing from red #FF5252 to bright yellow #FFF59D on each strike)
- **Sweat drop**: light blue (#40C4FF)

## Animation
- **Hammer-strike bounce (1s)**: rising stretch → at 50% it drops with a strong squash (scaleX 1.15, scaleY 0.85)
- **Right arm**: swings up from -45° to a 125° hammer-down
- **Eyes**: squeezed into thin lines at the moment of impact (48-60%)
- **Sparks**: three golden (#FFC107) sparks burst and scatter at the impact point
- **Sweat drop**: drifts up and to the left and fades out on a 2-second cycle

## Expression
Clawd, wearing a hard hat, strikes the anvil, sparks flying and sweat splashing.
