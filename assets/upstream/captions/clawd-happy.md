# clawd-happy.svg

A bouncing celebration animation of Clawd when extremely happy. 1s loop.

## Body
Full upright posture, with standard torso, four legs (1x2 each), both arms (2x2 each), and both eyes (1x2 each).

## Animation
- **Bounce (1s loop)**: Crouch down to prepare (scaleY 0.85) → spring up to -12px height (scaleY 1.05) → compress on landing (scaleY 0.85) → recover
- **Both arms waving**: Extremely fast alternation at 0.15s, left arm 45°-85°, right arm -45° to -85°
- **Ground shadow**: Changes in sync — enlarges on landing (scale 1.1), contracts on takeoff (scale 0.5, opacity 0.15)
- **Blink**: 2s cycle, a quick blink at about 50%
- **Pixel starlight**: 6 gold-yellow (#FFD700/#FFA000/#FFF59D/#FFC107) particles forming a cross-star sparkle on a 1.5s cycle

## Expression
Clawd bounces joyfully, surrounded by gold starlight, waving both arms at high speed.
